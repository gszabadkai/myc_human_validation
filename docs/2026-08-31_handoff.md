## 2. The science, in one place

**No hypothesis is supported.**

- **H1 falsified on both clauses.** Coupling (Block C): `MYC:OXPHOS` on PRIME
  0.020 / 0.010, percentile 31.6 of its matched null. Buffering (Block B E1): no
  quadrant enrichment, instruments opposite in sign.
- **H2, H3 not supported.** Every stratum flat; PI3K-intact the flattest.
- **Block D null**, on the forkscale stratum criterion 1 explicitly names. That
  closed criterion 1 on its complete list.
- **Block G failed at adequate power** - n = 1,130, CIs about +/- 0.04.
- **H4 not supported.** Pooled three-way is POSITIVE where negative was
  predicted; at I2 = 58% the random-effects estimate is +0.195 (-0.069, +0.459).
  The primary cohort (n = 988) sits at exactly zero.

**Three of four falsification criteria are met.** The fourth is unmet only
because criterion 4 demands an active *inversion*; H4 failed as a null.

**What survives:**

> `MYC x OXPHOS` associates with **lower BCL-XL and higher BIM**, PUMA unmoved,
> no compensating amplification. Block C, TCGA, transcript, **both instruments**.

BIM in DepMap is **UNRESOLVED** - breast n = 51 gives mitoPPS +0.107 (p 0.00018)
but GSVA +0.040 (p 0.055), so it never cleared the two-instrument rule. **Read
the Block G note section 5.1 before touching it**; "not universal, therefore
breast-specific" is the post-hoc rescue discipline forbids.

**Four results reported and NOT pursued:** `Glycine metabolism`, `Folate and

## 8. Two corrections carried forward, so they are not re-inherited

Both are recorded beside their registered results rather than folded into them,
and both are mine:

- **The BCL2L11 pan-cancer declaration** (Block G note section 5.1) contained a
  logically invalid rule: P1 pools 51 breast lines into 1,127, so a
  breast-confined effect dilutes inside P1's own CI. P1 was null under *both*
  hypotheses and could not have discriminated.
- **F1-b** (F1 note section 6) tests only the sign of a point estimate with no
  magnitude requirement, so -0.008 with a CI of -0.239 to +0.222 "passes". A
  coefficient indistinguishable from zero cannot corroborate a direction.

Neither changes a registered verdict. Both are the kind of error that looks like
a pass, which is why they are written down.
