## 2. The science, in one place

**No hypothesis is supported, and nothing survives replication.**

| | TCGA (n=938) | SCAN-B (n=3,143) |
|---|---|---|
| **PRIME** (the endpoint) | +0.020 (p 0.46), +0.010 (p 0.73) | not tested |
| **BBC3** (PUMA) | -0.014 (p 0.60), -0.034 (p 0.21) | -0.011 (p 0.52), -0.068 (p 7e-5) |
| **BCL2L1** (BCL-XL) | -0.034 (p 0.030), -0.043 (p 0.009) | -0.011 (p 0.14), -0.026 (p 6e-4) |
| **BCL2L11** (BIM) | **+0.051** (p 0.005), **+0.068** (p 3e-4) | **-0.036** (p 0.019), **-0.033** (p 0.037) |
| **MCL1** expression | +0.001 (p 0.94), +0.017 (p 0.33) | not tested |

(GSVA, then mitoPPS. All are the `MYC x OXPHOS` interaction.)

- **H1 falsified on both clauses.** Coupling null; no buffering enrichment.
- **H2, H3 not supported.** Every stratum flat, PI3K-intact the flattest.
- **Block D null**, closing criterion 1 on its complete stratum list.
- **Block G failed at adequate power** (n = 1,130, CIs about +/- 0.04).
- **H4 not supported.** Pooled three-way +0.195 (-0.069, +0.459), I2 58%; the
  n = 988 primary cohort sits at exactly zero.
- **The BIM replication FAILED, with the sign reversed.** I2 = 93% / 94% against
  the TCGA estimates, Q p 2.5e-4 / 3.8e-5, and **neither point estimate falls
  inside the other's CI.** The reversal beats 99.5% / 99.95% of
  expression-matched null sets, so it is specific, not a scale artefact.

**Three of four falsification criteria are met.** The fourth is unmet because it
names H4 and chemoresistance, and H4 failed as a null. **The BIM reversal does
NOT close criterion 4** - different endpoint, different criterion.

### The two things a reader will ask, answered

- **"Was SCAN-B underpowered?"** No - its CI is *narrower* than TCGA's (SE
  0.0155 vs 0.0181). But its 3,143 patients carry the information of about
  **1,285 TCGA patients**: the endpoint is noisier (sd 0.83 vs 0.58) and it has
  10.1% basal against TCGA's 17.0%. Lower yield per patient, more than
  compensated by size.
- **"Is the covariate reduction the explanation?"** No, and this was settled
  before SCAN-B was scored. Script 17's gate refit the TCGA limbs under the
  reduced SCAN-B covariate set on the same 938: the BIM estimate moved ~10% and
  kept sign and significance, with the full-spine estimate inside the reduced
  CI in every case.

**Five results reported and deliberately NOT pursued:** `Glycine metabolism`
(Block C), `Folate and 1-C` (Block B), `D MB2 DESCRIPTIVE log2_BCL2L1` (Block D),
**MCL1 in BrighTNess** (F1), and now **the negative BIM association in SCAN-B**.
The last is the strongest temptation in the whole arm and the most clearly
forbidden: it is a claim built backwards from a failed replication.

**Never test whether the sign returns in the basal or ER-negative subset.** It
is the obvious move, no subgroup was pre-specified for a reversal, and it would
be the least trustworthy number in the paper. It is already written into the
draft's limitations as a stated, untested possibility - which is where it stays.

## 9. Corrections carried forward, so they are not re-inherited

All mine, all recorded beside their results rather than folded in.

**A family, and the important one: criteria that test less than their label.**

- **F1-b** (F1 note section 6) tests only the sign of a point estimate with no
  magnitude requirement, so -0.008 with a CI of -0.239 to +0.222 "passes".
- **Condition 2 of the SCAN-B replication** (result note section 5) tests a
  two-sided `p < 0.05` beside a predicted direction, so it evaluated **met** on
  a result with the sign reversed. Condition 1 caught it and the verdict is
  right, but the table reads three of four met.

> **The rule this produces: a directional prediction needs a directional
> criterion. Writing `p < 0.05` next to a predicted sign does not make one.**
> Apply it to any future declaration in this project.

**Two others:**

- **The BCL2L11 pan-cancer declaration** (Block G note section 5.1) contained a
  logically invalid rule: P1 pools 51 breast lines into 1,127, so a
  breast-confined effect dilutes inside P1's own CI. P1 was null under *both*
  hypotheses and could not have discriminated.
- **Addendum A.3 of the SCAN-B declaration** claimed MitoCarta's synonyms could
  not reach the Felsher and Hallmark gene renames. They did - sheet 3 is the
  whole 19,247-gene human background, so the map has been a general HGNC alias
  source since 2026-08-28. Corrected in place; the discipline argument came out
  stronger, not weaker.
