**H2 and H3 are not supported.** Every stratum flat; PI3K-intact the flattest.

**The escape reading is not supported**, and its reversal is not mitochondrial.

**Block G's declared predictions FAIL - and this one is not a power excuse.**
G-a (MCL1) and G-b (BCL2L1) both fail. The lineage-adjusted pan-cancer fit is
n = 1,130 with CIs of about +/- 0.04 and every estimate sits on zero. Plan
section 10's "a negative result here weakens H1 considerably" is now cashed in.
G3 drug sensitivity is entirely null at n = 26-33, too small to mean anything.

**Three of four falsification criteria are met. Only H4 is untested.**

**What survives, and its exact status:**

> `MYC x OXPHOS` associates with **lower BCL-XL and higher BIM**, PUMA unmoved,
> no compensating amplification. That is Block C, TCGA, transcript, both
> instruments.

BIM in DepMap is **UNRESOLVED, not supported and not refuted**. Breast n = 51
gives mitoPPS +0.107 (p 0.00018) but GSVA only +0.040 (p 0.055), so it never
cleared the two-instrument rule. The declared pan-cancer check established that
**the effect is not universal** (P2, 1,076 non-breast lines, zero on both
instruments, CIs a tenth of the breast estimate) but **could not** establish
whether the breast result is real - see the next paragraph.

**Read `docs/2026-08-30_block_g_result_and_bcl2l11_declaration.md` section 5.1
before touching this.** The declaration contained a logically invalid rule,
written by Claude: it said a null P1 would show the breast coefficient was
noise. It cannot. P1 pools 51 breast lines into 1,127, so a breast-confined
effect dilutes to ~0.005, inside P1's own CI. P1 was null under both hypotheses.
The registered verdict ("noise, drop it") stands as registered; the correction
is recorded beside it, not folded in. **No claim of breast-specificity is
licensed** - "not universal, therefore breast-specific" is the post-hoc rescue
that discipline forbids.

**Two results reported and deliberately NOT pursued.** `Glycine metabolism`
(Block C) and `Folate and 1-C` (Block B). Neither has a prior. Chasing either is
the fifth post-hoc hypothesis plan section 2 forbids.

**One live commitment.** The BIM replication declared in the Block C note
