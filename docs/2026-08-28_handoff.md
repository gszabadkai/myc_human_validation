**Two framing points that changed this session, from reading the mouse source:**

- The mouse OXPHOS decline is **age-related, not MYC-driven** - it falls on both
  timelines and the genotypes do not differ (p = 0.615). What is MYC-specific is
  that Foxo3 fails to rise and the priming ratio collapses. The coupling **flips
  sign** with genotype (WT -4.80, Myc+ +4.35), so the interaction is the right
  test and there is no main effect to add.
- The mouse interaction is **a lead, not a confirmed result** - its own scripts
  say so twice. It fails its permutation null (p = 0.083) and weakens with a
  timepoint term (0.0052 -> 0.088), at n = 6 per cell. And the specificity claim
  rests on **one comparator**. So the human arm is the adequately-powered first
  test, not a confirmation exercise. That is a stronger position, but it changes
  what a human null means and should be said in the text.
