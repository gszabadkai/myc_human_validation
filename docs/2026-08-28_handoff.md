## Sign-off needed before script 07 is sourced

Four things the script decides that its approved spec did not cover. All four
are flagged in the script itself at the point they happen.

1. **The mitoPPS universe excludes the mouse's `Apoptosis-PRO` / `Apoptosis-ANTI`
   pathways** (script section 5.1). Two independent reasons: those lists carry
   `Bbc3` and `Bcl2l1`, whose human orthologs are PRIME's numerator and
   denominator, so they would put the endpoint's genes into the exposure's
   construction; and they are a mouse-curated set, so uppercasing them into
   human is the ortholog projection CLAUDE.md forbids. MitoCarta's own
   human-native `Apoptosis` pathway (35 genes) stays in. Consequence: the human
   and mouse mitoPPS universes differ by two pathways.
2. **GSVA `minSize = 3`, not 10.** Forced by the per-complex sets the plan
   requires - `Complex II` is 4 genes and `ATPase` is 5. Every set below 10
   genes is flagged `small` in each output table. Script 06 used 10 and is
   unaffected.
3. **Null draws are without replacement within a ventile.** The mouse drew with
   replacement, which is harmless for a mean of per-gene statistics but would
   silently shrink a GSVA set, because GSVA de-duplicates set members.
4. **Two descriptive instruments are computed alongside the two co-primary
   ones** - `content` (log2 sum of linear counts) and `zmean` (mean z of VST).
   They are the mouse's other two weightings and cost nothing. No claim rests on
   them and the "claim only what both support" rule still refers to GSVA and
   mitoPPS only.

## What script 07 does

Per the RESOLVED proposal and the amended plan:

1. **OXPHOS on BOTH instruments, co-primary.**
   - GSVA level on the VST matrix (`kcdf = "Gaussian"`) - the instrument that
     travels across cohorts, so Block F uses it.
   - mitoPPS on the LINEAR matrix - the instrument the mouse interaction was
     fitted on. TCGA-internal only; never compared across cohorts.
   - **Rule: report both, claim only what BOTH support.** One-instrument effects
     are instrument-dependent, not positive results.
2. **The specificity arms**, from Human MitoCarta 3.0 by pathway name, each
   answering a named alternative. `OXPHOS assembly factors` (68) is the PRIMARY
   negative. `Carnitine shuttle` (5) is merged into `Fatty acid oxidation` (44).
3. **2,000 expression-matched random gene sets** as the null for every arm.
4. **Per-complex sets** from `data/genesets_metabolic_human/` (Complex I 49,
   II 4, III 11, IV 27, ATPase 5) - MitoCarta has no complex breakdown and the
   mouse claim is "across all complexes".
5. **Both D7 proliferation covariates**: `PROLIF_STD` (327 genes) and
   `PROLIF_DISJOINT` (318 - the 9 shared with stripped Felsher removed).
   `PROLIF_DISJOINT` is defined against M-a and **must never be used with M-b**.
6. mtDNA-encoded OXPHOS held separately, per standing convention.

Traps handled in the script, listed so a reader knows they were not missed:

- the two matrices never meet, and both assert their own `scale` field;
- symbols are rescued through MitoCarta's Sheet-3 synonyms **towards the matrix
  vocabulary** - the opposite direction from G1, which harmonised towards
  MitoCarta. `KARS` -> `KARS1` cost G1 a gene until it was caught;
- the map is built once over the union of all symbols and applied per
  collection, because several arm names are also MitoPathway names and the
  merged `Fatty acid oxidation` arm is NOT the `Fatty acid oxidation` pathway.
  Keying anything by set name across collections gives one the other's genes;
- every GSVA call carries two complementary half-matrix pin sets, so a call with
  20 pathways and a call with 2,000 random sets walk the same gene universe. The
  observed arm is re-scored inside its own null batch and the script stops if
  the value moves at all;
- the mitoPPS matrix rewrite is asserted against the definitional triple-loop
  form, and every arm that is a universe pathway must reproduce its canonical
  value exactly;
- the per-complex sets are checked against MitoCarta's nuclear OXPHOS subunits,
  which are an independent curation of the same genes. Note the source list is
  internally inconsistent about mtDNA - `Complex I` lists all seven `MT-ND`
  genes and `Complex III` lists `MT-CYB`, while `Complex IV` and `ATPase` list
  none - so the mtDNA strip is what makes the five sets comparable, not a
  formality.

## Two decisions open - they block 09, NOT 07

**1. Missing-data policy.** Proposed in the `03` header, unsigned: complete cases
for the primary Block C model (938 of 1,095), n reported with every estimate, no
imputation - the missingness is in covariates rather than exposure or endpoint,
is driven by assay coverage, and imputing PAM50 or plate would invent the
structure the design depends on. Plus a pre-specified sensitivity refit on all
patients using only fully-observed covariates.

**2. Plate handling.** 43 levels for 938 complete cases, several at n = 1-2
(`A22O` 1, `A26B` 1, `A466` 1, `A10U` 2, `A32Y` 2). As a fixed factor those cost
42 df and contribute nothing. Options: pool < 10 into `other`, fit as a random
effect, or drop.

**Two framing points that changed this session, from reading the mouse source:**

- The mouse OXPHOS decline is **age-related, not MYC-driven** - it falls on both
  timelines and the genotypes do not differ (p = 0.615). What is MYC-specific is
  that Foxo3 fails to rise and the priming ratio collapses. The coupling **flips
  sign** with genotype (WT -4.80, Myc+ +4.35), so the interaction is the right
  test and there is no main effect to add.
- The mouse interaction is **a lead, not a confirmed result** - its own scripts
  say so twice. It fails its permutation null (p = 0.083) and weakens with a
  timepoint term (0.0052 -> 0.088), at n = 6 per cell. And the specificity claim
  rests on **one comparator**. So the human arm is the adequately-powered first
  test, not a confirmation exercise. That is a stronger position, but it changes
  what a human null means and should be said in the text.
