# G2 result - CNV co-occurrence in TCGA-BRCA

Run 2026-08-28 via `scripts/05_gate_cnv_cooccurrence.R`, figure via
`scripts/05b_plot_g2_gate.R`. Results in `results/g2_cnv_cooccurrence.rds`,
tables in `outputs/tables/g2_*.csv`, figure at
`outputs/figures/g2_gate_forest.{pdf,png}`.

Design, thresholds and pass criterion were fixed before the statistic was
computed: see `2026-08-28_D4_scoping_and_G2_design.md` sections 2.3, 2.6, 2.8.

---

## 1. Verdict

**G2 PASSES.** Per plan section 6: **H1 becomes Panel b and H4 gains its
stratifying variable.** H2 does not move to primary; Panel b is not the
FOXO3/PI3K decoupling figure.

The partners split, as anticipated. Per the pre-registered rule,
**`BCL2L1` carries Panel b**; `MCL1` is reported as replication of Lee et al.
2017; `BBC3` is a pre-specified negative with its control.

## 2. Primary tests (ISAR, aneuploidy-adjusted, n = 1,043)

| Partner | Rule | n both | unadj OR | **adj OR** | 95% CI | p | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `MCL1` | `+2` | 53 | 1.77 | **1.69** | 1.17 - 2.44 | 0.0050 | **PASS** |
| `BCL2L1` | `>= +1` | 132 | 1.93 | **1.77** | 1.26 - 2.47 | 0.00090 | **PASS** |
| `BBC3` | `<= -1` | 66 | 1.46 | 1.36 | 0.966 - 1.91 | 0.075 | FAIL |
| `BAX` (control) | `<= -1` | 61 | - | 1.41 | 0.986 - 1.99 | 0.057 | control |

Aneuploidy shrinkage was small throughout (1.77->1.69, 1.93->1.77,
1.46->1.36). ISAR plus conditioning did its job: **the confound was real enough
to require handling but is not what drives the result.** Report the unadjusted
and adjusted values side by side anyway.

## 3. BBC3 fails, and fails cleanly - the control earned its place

```
BBC3   adj OR 1.36   CI 0.966 - 1.91   p = 0.075
BAX    adj OR 1.41   CI 0.986 - 1.99   p = 0.057
```

`BBC3` is **indistinguishable from its 19q13 regional control.** The marginal
signal is arm-level 19q behaviour, not PUMA-specific.

This matters because in source B (Firehose) `BBC3` returns 1.57 (1.10 - 2.22)
and would have "passed". **The pre-specified `BAX` control is the only thing
standing between that number and an over-read.** Had BBC3 been left as
descriptive-only per the original design, or had the control not been added,
this would have been reported wrongly.

The reading stands as written in the design note: **the CNV route to
neutralising `PRIME` exists on the buffer side and is effectively absent on the
trigger side. Tumours do not delete PUMA; they amplify the sponge.** Homozygous
`BBC3` deletion is 5 of 1,043 and its joint count with MYC amplification is
zero.

## 4. Threshold sensitivity - MCL1 inverts at gain level

MYC held at `+2` throughout:

| Partner | Rule | n both | adj OR | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `MCL1` | `+2` | 53 | 1.69 | 1.17 - 2.44 |
| `MCL1` | `>= +1` | 157 | **0.503** | **0.353 - 0.719** |
| `BCL2L1` | `>= +1` | 132 | 1.77 | 1.26 - 2.47 |
| `BCL2L1` | `+2` | 16 | **5.41** | 2.49 - 12.2 |
| `BBC3` | `<= -1` | 66 | 1.36 | 0.966 - 1.91 |
| `BBC3` | `-2` | 0 | degenerate | separated |

Two things to carry:

- **`MCL1` inverts at `>= +1`.** MYC-amplified tumours are *less* likely to
  carry MCL1 gain. So the MYC/MCL1 co-amplification story is specifically about
  **high-level** amplification and reverses at gain level. **Treat as a caveat
  needing scrutiny, not as a finding:** MCL1 gain is 76% of the cohort, and
  adjusting for aneuploidy against a near-saturated marker can manufacture a
  negative. Do not put this in text before it is understood.
- **`BCL2L1` is stronger at `+2` (5.41) than at `>= +1` (1.77)**, on 16 events.
  Consistent direction, wide CI. The pre-registered primary remains `>= +1`.

GUARD, unchanged: ORs at different thresholds are **not** comparable across
genes. Compare within a threshold only.

## 5. The association is concentrated in Basal

PAM50 strata, primary rule per gene:

| Partner | Basal | Her2 | LumA | LumB |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `MCL1` | **3.12** (1.53-6.56) | 1.22 | 0.68 | 1.12 |
| `BCL2L1` | **2.69** (1.33-5.62) | 0.99 | 1.70 | 0.90 |
| `BBC3` | **0.50** (0.257-0.971) | 1.05 | 1.65 | 1.10 |

Breslow-Day homogeneity:

| Partner | by PAM50 | by TNBC |
|---|---|---|
| `MCL1` | 0.051 (4 strata) | **0.022** |
| `BCL2L1` | 0.327 | 0.846 |
| `BBC3` | 0.200 | 0.316 |

**`MCL1`'s association is genuinely basal-specific; `BCL2L1`'s is broad.** That
is a real difference between the replication arm and the novel arm, and it is
what Panel b can carry: BCL2L1 buffering is not confined to the subtype Lee et
al. studied.

`BBC3` loss is *negatively* associated with MYC amplification within Basal
(0.50, CI excludes 1). Given section 3, do not interpret this as a finding
without first ruling out the same 19q13 regional effect within-stratum.

## 6. Co-altered group enrichment for Basal / TNBC - with a caveat

| Partner | Target | n | co-altered | OR | 95% CI | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `MCL1` | Basal | 969 | 52 | 9.94 | 5.31 - 19.07 | 2.4e-14 |
| `BCL2L1` | Basal | 969 | 124 | 4.48 | 2.92 - 6.87 | 3.0e-12 |
| `BBC3` | Basal | 969 | 62 | 3.31 | 1.84 - 5.87 | 3.6e-5 |
| `MCL1` | TNBC | 904 | 42 | 5.51 | 2.78 - 10.95 | 3.9e-7 |
| `BCL2L1` | TNBC | 904 | 115 | 2.75 | 1.72 - 4.34 | 1.4e-5 |
| `BBC3` | TNBC | 904 | 55 | 3.07 | 1.63 - 5.66 | 2.9e-4 |

This answers plan section 6's question as written, and the answer is a strong
yes. **But the test as constructed is partly definitional and must not be
reported as a bare enrichment.** The co-altered group is a subset of the
MYC-amplified group, and MYC amplification is itself strongly basal-enriched, so
some of every row above is MYC's own subtype distribution.

The `BBC3` row demonstrates it concretely: the co-altered group is Basal-enriched
at OR 3.31, while `BBC3` loss *within* Basal is protective at OR 0.50. Those are
not contradictory - the first is carried by MYC amplification, not by BBC3 loss.

**PROPOSED, not yet run:** repeat the enrichment conditioning on MYC
amplification - co-altered versus MYC-amplified-but-not-co-altered. That isolates
the partner's contribution. Cheap, and it should be added before any Panel b
figure is drawn.

## 7. Robustness

**Source B agrees in direction on all three** (pre-registered requirement met):

|---|---|---|---|
| `MCL1` | 1.69 (1.17-2.44) | 1.85 (1.25-2.72) | 1.84 (1.24-2.72) |

B and B-intersection are nearly identical, so the 37-patient difference between
the sample sets is not doing any work; the A/B difference is the **calling
method**, as intended.

**Covariate swap - one caveat that must travel with the BCL2L1 result:**

| Partner | ANEUPLOIDY_SCORE | FRACTION_GENOME_ALTERED |
|---|---|---|
| `MCL1` | 1.69 (1.17-2.44) | 1.60 (1.10-2.31) |
| `BCL2L1` | 1.77 (1.26-2.47) | **1.36 (0.984-1.88)** |
| `BBC3` | 1.36 (0.966-1.91) | 1.12 (0.792-1.58) |

Under FGA, **`BCL2L1` drops below the line.** Both covariates were pre-specified,
so this is a disclosed sensitivity rather than a choice - but **BCL2L1's pass is
covariate-dependent and cannot be reported as a bare odds ratio.** Report both.

Sample rule verified: 1,043 `-01` and 7 `-06` in the ISAR BRCA subset, i.e. the
sample-type filter removed exactly the 7 metastases it was designed to remove.

## 8. What this means for the manuscript

With D4 resolved as option 1, the division of labour is now explicit:

- **MYC/MCL1 high-level co-amplification, basal-restricted** - replication of
  Lee et al. 2017. Cited, not claimed.
- **MYC/BCL2L1 co-alteration, subtype-broad** - novel. `BCL2L1` is the `PRIME`
  denominator and the term Lee et al. never touch. **This is Panel b.**
- **No trigger-side CNV route** - `BBC3` loss is not associated with MYC
  amplification once its regional control is applied. Supports the buffering
  model over a trigger-loss model, and is reportable as a negative because the
  control was pre-specified.

G2 does **not** establish the OXPHOS or priming link. It establishes that the
buffering lesion is there and non-random. The interaction on `PRIME` is still
entirely open and needs scripts 01, 06, 07, 08, 09.
